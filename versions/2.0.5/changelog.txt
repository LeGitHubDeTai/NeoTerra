# 📦 NeoTerra - Changelog v2.0.5

---

## 🆕 Additions

### ⚔️ New Bonuses & Maluses

- **Azmador**
  - Malus **Unbearable Heat**: Takes burn damage every second while under the sun or near a heat source (10 to 15 sec).

- **Ophydros**
  - Bonus **Aquatic Regeneration**: Health regenerates 2x faster underwater.
  - Malus **Aquatic Storms**: Suffers Slowness or Mining Fatigue during rain.
  - Bonus **Underwater Breathing**: Breathe underwater indefinitely.

- **Skaeldur**
  - Bonus **Accelerated Extraction**: Mining speed greatly increased (2 min).
  - Bonus **Day’s Blessing**: Gain Regeneration I while standing in sunlight (1.5 to 2 min).
  - Bonus **Berserker**: Gain Strength I for 5 sec when under 4 hearts.
  - Malus **Eternal Chill**: Permanently suffers from Slowness I.
  - Malus **Clumsiness**: 20% chance to drop your weapon when attacking.
  - Malus **Bottomless Stomach**: Hunger drains 2x faster than normal.
  - Malus **Night Skin**: Blindness when in darkness (light level < 5).
  - Malus **Fragile Bones**: Takes 1.5x more fall damage.

- **Sylthar**
  - Malus **Natural Balance**: Killing passive mobs (cow, pig, etc.) summons a spider or silverfish near you.
  - Malus **Natural Rhythm**: Cannot sprint at night. Gains Slowness II at night.
  - Malus **Chaotic Metamorphosis**: Randomly becomes tiny or giant.
  - Malus **Natural Fragility**: Suffers Weakness I when exposed to daylight.
  - Malus **Earth’s Disease**: Poisonous plants/spores may poison the player.
  - Malus **Telluric Force**: The higher you are, the weaker you get (Slowness & Mining Fatigue above Y=100).
  - Bonus **Nature's Healing**: Heals nearby players when near vegetation or animals.

- **General**
  - Bonus **Speed on Fire**: Gain Speed while burning.
  - Eating **soup** reverts you to normal size if tiny or giant.

- Boss name no longer appears if player dies during a raid.

  - Purgatory
  - Nether
- Fixed sand in **Redstone Challenge** (chunk load issue).
- Mace breaks faster (was being overused).
- Fixed **TP after Purgatory** (multiple players now return correctly).
- Performance improvements:
  - Reduced lag from **fishing rod sounds**
  - Optimized reward/effect handling
- You can now **whip all entities**.
- Players **teleport back 1 block above** bed (prevents getting stuck).
- **God titles** only display in front of statues (Overworld only).
