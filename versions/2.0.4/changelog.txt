# 📜 Changelog NeoTerra 2.0.4

## 🆕 ADDITIONS:

- **New mod**: Inventory Management mod.
- **Added a rare white fox** that drops a rare offering.
- **New crafting recipes**:
  - Diamond horse armor.
  - Gold horse armor.
  - Leather horse armor.
  - Iron horse armor.
  - Endstone.
  - Calcite.
- **The stonecutter now deals damage** if the player stands on it.
- **Added a 6th raid wave**, featuring a **random boss**.
- **Added 3 new bosses** in the 6th wave.
- **New resource packs added**:
  - Enchant Icons.
  - Enchant Icons Colorless.
  - Enchanted Books from Mars.
  - Fresh Animations.
  - Dramatic Skys.
- **New shaders added**:
  - Aurora's Shaders.
  - Insanity Shader.
  - BSL Shaders.
  - MakeUp - Ultra Fast.
- **Added a slime chunk button** in MiniHUD-Seed in the Helper GUI (to easily locate slime chunks).
- **Added a tutorial** on the website for Bedrock players.
- **Added poop mechanics**:
  - If the player eats, **within 1-2 hours they will defecate**.
  - Poop can be **eaten** or **composted**.
- **Added chat messages** (FR/EN) for all rewards from:
  - Azmador.
  - Ophydros.
  - Skaeldur.
  - Sylthar.
- **Added a new debuff: "Diarrhea"**.
- **Complete rebalance of rewards**.
- **Added particle effects** for the **Fear of Predators** effect.
- **Drinking milk** now **removes all effects**.
- **Added a piss jar**:
  - If the player drinks, **within 1-2 hours they will urinate**.
  - The piss jar can be **drunk** or **thrown**.
  - **Effects based on usage**:
    - If drunk **within 10 minutes** → **Healing effect**.
    - If drunk **after 10 minutes** → **Nausea effect**.
    - If thrown → **Nausea on the ground + Instant damage**.
- **Added a new debuff: "Uncontrollable Urination"**.
- **Added a search bar** on the wiki.
- **Added explanations for macOS crashes** (see wiki).
- **Added a storm cloud**.
- **Added new helper tools** in the **F3 menu**.
- **Added new food items**:
  - **Lasagna** (craftable & edible).
  - **Muffin** (craftable & edible).
- **Added a new debuff for Skaeldur**: **Cursed Blood**.
- **Added new sounds**:
  - **When a raid starts**.
  - **When a raid ends**.
- **Added two new whips**:
  - **Normal whip** → Deals **1 heart of damage**.
  - **Vine whip** → Applies **Poison for 10 seconds**.

## ❌ REMOVALS:

- **Removed anvil** from Skaeldur's loot table.
- **Removed Evokers** from raid **waves 4 and 5**.
- **Removed the command** `/trigger NeoTerra_lang`.

## 🛠️ FIXES:

- **Raids no longer disappear** if the player dies.
- **Fixed server crash** when players were in the Redstone challenge.
- **Added sand** in the Redstone challenge.
- **The "Flooding" curse is now paused** if the player is in purgatory.
- **Fixed a softlock bug** where players returning from purgatory with the "Flooding" curse could be stuck in bedrock at spawn.
- **Azmador’s statue now rotates correctly** in purgatory (statues are now aligned).
- **Armor stands are no longer visible** when using the mace.
- **Fixed tool durability** on **dirt paths**.
- **Fixed durability issues** on the mace.
- **The 3x3 mace now breaks blocks faster** (turns into a "highway").
- **The mace has regained its intended function** (crushing attack).
- **Fixed the "Depth’s Plague" debuff**:
  - It **dealt too much damage**.
  - It **was not triggering correctly while swimming**.
- **Fixed Azmador’s lightning-related debuffs**:
  - **Wrath of Lightning**.
  - **Thunderous Judgment**.
- **All custom crafting recipes are now visible** in the recipe book.
- **Armor stands in "Find the Button" are no longer bugged**.
- **Raid fireworks no longer kill players** (replaced with particles).
- **If there is only one raid wave, the next waves are no longer delayed**.
